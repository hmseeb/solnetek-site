About Us

Mission-Ready Cybersecurity, Built for Compliance

Solnetek is an Atlanta-based cybersecurity and compliance consulting firm led by a Licensed Cybersecurity Compliance Advisor (LCCA). We specialize in helping Defense Industrial Base (DIB) contractors, government agencies, and small businesses build the security programs they need to win contracts, pass audits, and protect sensitive data.

Our team brings hands-on expertise in CMMC, NIST frameworks, penetration testing, and enterprise security architecture. We don't just check boxes — we build lasting cybersecurity programs that align with your mission and grow with your organization.

Whether you're preparing for your first CMMC assessment, trying to understand your risk posture, or building a security program from scratch, Solnetek is the partner who gets it done.
